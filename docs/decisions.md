# VectorBuilder: decisions in force

Rules binding now, digested from the design spec; its body is authoritative and its [§12] holds the history.

## What it is
- VectorBuilder is a general vector editor inspired by Illustrator and Figma (an inspiration, never a specification or a feature checklist: owner, 2026-10-12), with extra options for consoles (data, logic, pages, devices, export); it is not built around the owner's consoles or any one design system (owner, 2026-10-11). The owner's consoles and the fixtures made from them are examples: judge behaviour, never how an example looks. A starting set of widgets, editable once placed, and complete themes (provided, and from M5 imported from design systems) sit on top; a widget's look, shapes included, comes from the theme, never from options copied from particular consoles; more widgets come after 1.0 (owner, 2026-10-12). [§1] [§5.1] [§5.2]

## E1, the editing milestone (owner, 2026-10-12; implementation integrated, before M5)
- Looks from the theme: every widget paints through the theme's parts (paint, outline width and placement, radius, shadow, ink) and lays out by its metrics; the Panel's `look` and `marks` options go; default texts are neutral. [§1] [§5.1] [§5.2]
- A theme may change a widget's shapes through its anatomy (owner, 2026-10-12, E1 plan OQ2: "yes", for design systems imported in M5, "not necesaly for my design"): per hook (each paint part, each type style's labels, the toggle's switch track, the glyph's box; `<widget type>/<hook>` for one widget), shapes added to a part, its shape replaced or removed. Built in E1 so M5 only maps onto it; no provided theme uses it in E1, and the owner's designs are not made into themes. Shapes are placed by `[at, by]` edges on the part's box (CSS `calc`); fills cannot cross themselves (rectangles, ellipses, corner cuts, triangles, polygons convex at any size); point lists stay literal; no paths, text, pictures, clips, masks or rotation. A label keeps its text and its ornaments draw under it; a part showing a value by its size or place is never removed; a removed shape stays as an unpainted node. Anatomy is outside input once imported: one sanitizer (known keys, null-prototype records, finite float32 numbers, the theme's own tokens), and the dresser writes only keys each op reads. [§2 D11] [§5.2]
- Provided themes: **Neutral** (the default; the `T` font left out, so the game's own face; no Fonts mod for widget-only exports) and **Vanilla +** (output unchanged by the move into parts). The five recoloured presets are removed; another provided theme comes only as a complete design. Required roles are the 26 UI roles. A theme id this build lacks draws in the default theme, with a notice. [§2 D11] [§5.2]
- Gas colours are game facts shared by every theme, held outside the themes; a theme may restyle their use, never the gas colours. Exact values and source are open. Vanilla +'s 17 `gas-*` tokens are its own, not required, not game facts. [§5.2]
- Placed widgets stay editable (owner, 2026-10-12, E1 plan OQ1: "default", instance overrides): a part's paint, effects, position and size are overrides kept in the marker (`__edit`), surviving parameter edits, theme switches, copies and loads; untouched parts follow the theme; structure on one instance still needs Detach; overrides never touch ids, events, clips, masks or text. A theme's ornaments are parts (`<part>~<ornament>`): their paint and visibility are overrides, their place follows their part; a part the theme removes can be painted back on one instance. Ordinary groups may hold widgets; widgets never nest. A code-pane edit inside a widget still detaches it. [§4] [§5.1]

## Versions and pin
- Pin the vector mod by tag, to the newest tag pushed to origin. Re-pin only when the current work is done, or after asking the owner (2026-10-06); between re-pins, upstream's changes wait in a running list. The next re-pin is the last step before the public deployment, to the newest tag then, with its own review and in-game check (owner, 2026-10-12: "Can the repin be right before deployment?"). The current pin is `v0.11.118` (`1f877d7a3682fc61cec364427d590d6400319ecb`). [§3]
- The export floor is the pin: no fixed minimum, no per-feature gating. The only guard is each page's old-mod banner `G v="=lt(ver,FLOOR)"` (FLOOR = the pin's `ver`, 11118). Since 0.11.103, `ver` is `major*1000000 + minor*1000 + patch`; the newest-tag floor/banner stays even while Workshop publication is unchecked. [§2] [§6.3]
- Never wait for upstream: a vector bug goes upstream with a repro, then counts as solved. No workaround, guard or trap warning for it, and no test that depends on it before the fixed tag is pinned. [§3]
- Generated Lua is Lua 5.2; the interpreter is Lua-CSharp `d9fb336`, the game's own. [§2] [§3]

## Limits and caps
- No cap of our own on authored depth, symbol instances or expanded nodes: parse, print, validate, numbering and scene checks are iterative (explicit stacks, no path per node). Limits exist only for wrong input (a `SYM` cycle is refused) and real external limits. [§4]
- Scenes go to `ss.ui` as `src` text, so nothing relies on how deep ScriptedScreens' Lua-table conversion can go: that limit is unproven either way ("about 2000 nested tables" was a vector 0.11.81 measurement). Table props stay shallow, never nested deeper than 64, as a margin. [§6.4]
- Scene checks run in a worker with a timeout. Work budgets only protect responsiveness and report "too large to check here", never "invalid". [§4]

## ScriptedScreens and host
- ScriptedScreens flushes at `ui:commit()` and at the end of every chip execution (the load, each tick). Within one execution an element's writes arrive as one payload, its merged state: `set_props` merges ordinary props per key (a prop not named again survives, the declaration's included); the table-valued props `data`, `nodes` and `ease` are each replaced whole (`keep=1` cannot save the earlier table; `data` then `nodes` do not collide); a second declaration of one id replaces the first whole (omitting `scene` sends it elsewhere). Two writes to one element in one execution carrying the same table prop lose the first table unless `ui:commit()` runs between; declare at load + patch in tick is safe. The exporter commits between such writes and never declares one id twice in an execution. Cross-surface commit scope is provisional, unprobed; compare two surfaces before the end-of-execution flush. Props persist across executions: `snap=1` written once stays on until `snap=0`, and `ease` persists the same way (measured). Since vector 0.11.104, absent `data` is inert: a `nodes`-only payload does not restart `since()`. Explicit empty `data` still clears; an explicitly present retained table is still a delivery. Geometry, snapped data and eased data remain separate builder elements. Captures cannot measure glides or `since()`. [§6.4]
- The fake host models that merge (flush = `ui:commit()` or the end of any chip execution: the load and each tick). Its bounded lost-write diagnostic stays at the shared flush boundary for runtime and export tests; it is not a preview status field. A host device sharing the housing ref is skipped with a warning while the remaining configuration loads; malformed JSON and duplicate device refs still reject the host. It is written from the public docs; never copy decompiled code. [§7]
- Exports use only public StationeersLua and ScriptedScreens APIs; beta extras are feature-detected. [§2]

## Rendering
- WebGL 2 follows vector v0.11.118: float straight sRGB (UV2) interpolates between vertices and decodes per fragment; opacity composites in linear light through an sRGB8 intermediate, preserving dark vector flats. Native UI and picture tint retain the byte-linear vertex path. The scene target clears transparent: "console without a bg assigned have NO background"; the owner chooses "checkerboard" (2026-10-13). Inside the page, unpainted areas display #282828/#303030 in 8 CSS px squares independent of zoom; the workspace keeps its own background. Scene and cover RGB composite over the checker in linear light. Only assigned page backgrounds produce native panel content. An undefined background exports no bg panel and previews as the checkerboard through empty and translucent areas; the Devices page inherits the first page's background, including none. New pages keep their theme ground. Design → Page offers No background (one undo step), colour and vertical gradient; save/load and theme switches preserve absence. readPixels and CaptureOverlay read only the scene target, empty alpha 0; comparison ignores RGB when both pixels are transparent. The former #0A121C stand-in was never an established game colour. Labels are DOM text in the real faces; under ztext a shape drawn after a label covers it, through WebGL covers among the labels (owner, 2026-10-06; built): in each covered label's region (its box, laid-out text and per-line glyph ink measured in the real face with its kerning and spacing, past the box under fit 0 or 2, grown by its shadows and outline; fit 1 clips to the box) a cover shows the canvas's colour at the alpha of what is drawn after the label, from the same sRGB8 target, premultiplied. The labels and the covers stack in the game's draw order: a label drawn after a covering shape stays over it, covers of labels drawn earlier go in deeper layers (canvases of their own), and stacked covers multiply. Exact for opaque covers wherever a label's ink lies in its region; the measure includes glyph overhangs and accents beyond DOM line boxes, with rotation and shear. Real font baselines still need a browser check; 200-label update and late-face costs are recorded in `docs/m2c.md`. Under a translucent cover the text shows through by exactly what the geometry leaves, but in the canvas's colour, blended by the browser in sRGB. The earlier capture measured about 30 levels per channel darker than the game at alpha 0.5 (16 to 35 from 0.25 to 0.85); that DOM-text limitation remains, and v0.11.118 needs a new matched-scale capture. The core sends its box-based `cover`, each label's eligible `after` vertex, and the element's shape starts and bounds; the preview can move the cut earlier where a later shape overlaps measured ink outside the T box. Picking uses that displayed cut. Unchanged label extents are cached; only text/font/layout changes are measured, while font loading/import invalidates only labels using that face (including tags and weight slots); zoom and surface-size changes invalidate all. ztext=0 keeps a label over its own element's shapes, and every later element is drawn over it. [§4] [§7]
- Any canvas that draws vector geometry needs TexCoord1 for edge coverage and TexCoord2 for float sRGB. A capture is a camera render of a cloned tree on a fresh canvas. [§8]

## Fonts
- ScriptedScreensFonts 0.3.0.0 supplies exactly 36 faces: Barlow and Barlow Condensed, each with nine upright weights and their italics (source-verified 2026-10-11). Regular is the bare family; other names append Thin, ExtraLight, Light, Medium, SemiBold, Bold, ExtraBold or Black; italics append ` Italic`. Manrope and Inter require custom import. Liberation Sans is the preview fallback; vector T defaults to LiberationSans SDF with the synthetic styles below. [§5.3]
- Slots come from metadata style: Thin/Hairline 1, ExtraLight/UltraLight 2, Light 3, empty/Regular 4, Medium 5, SemiBold/DemiBold 6, Bold 7, ExtraBold/UltraBold 8, Black/Heavy 9; other styles 0. Remove ITALIC for slot lookup while recording italic; Oblique is not implicitly italic. Only `<b>` and `<font-weight=N>` move the weight slot (initially 4); naming a face chooses glyphs only. `<i>` requests that weight's italic: a real italic or Bold Italic draws as is. Without an italic face, shear the current glyph with unchanged advances; without Bold Italic, keep the current glyph, add faux bold and shear. A missing bold slot alone adds faux bold without shear. Thus Barlow Bold plus `<i>` selects Barlow Italic, while Barlow plus `<b><i>` selects Barlow Bold Italic. The no-font vector T has no linked slots, so bold and italic are synthetic. [§5.3]
- TMP metrics live in one per-face table: vector default LiberationSans SDF and every Fonts-mod face shear by 0.35; `<i=angle>` overrides it with angle/100, clamped to ±180. Faux bold adds an outward contour offset of 0.0196 em (default) or 0.0195 em (Fonts mod) without an outline, drawn with twice that stroke width under the fill; spacing adds 0.07 times the base size per character. CSS weight and italic synthesis are disabled. The DOM shears shaped runs around their centres, preserving advances and kerning; exact per-glyph centres remain a gap. [§5.3]
- Preview outlines mirror the author's shipped vector 0.11.113 formula, seen in game (2026-10-11); the pin is 0.11.118 and outlines are drawn by the preview, not the vendored core. Default gs/pointSize = 10/86; Fonts-mod = 6/48; other = max(0,0.75)/4 = 0.1875. For target = ow*pointSize/(2*max(1,size)), W = target/(gs-1) through target <= (gs-1)*(1-other), else target*other/(gs-1-target) below gs-1, else 1; clamp W to 1. Outward(W) = W*(gs-1)/max(1,other+W) atlas px; ScaleRatioA = (gs-1)/(gs*max(1,other+W)). Faux bold scales by ScaleRatioA/((gs-1)/gs), unchanged through W = 0.8125 (ow 14.625 at size 86 or 8.125 at size 48). Spacing is unchanged. When W clamps, the centred stroke uses twice Outward(1)*size/pointSize and the label warns “the outline is reduced to N%”, N = 100*Outward(1)/target, displayed to one decimal. The caps at those sizes are 288/19 and 160/19 scene units; clamping starts before target = gs-1. Font-tag runs use their own calibration and the label base size. [§5.3]
- Every source glyph draws; missing codepoints use browser fallback without a warning. GPOS kerning and browser shaping stay intact. Relative `<size>` forms use the label base size; plain/px sizes are absolute and closing tags restore the prior size. Attribute `cspace` and faux-bold spacing use the base size; tag `<cspace>` is absolute scene units or em of the current size, with no trailing tag gap; percentages are literal. Other unimplemented styling tags are stripped; br becomes a newline. Shrink fit still uses the requested size in the preview, so its auto-sized base spacing remains a gap. [§5.3]
- All 36 default faces and Liberation Sans regular are self-hosted OFL `.woff2`, with their licences. Register lazily: no fetch until text uses a face; bundled faces need no third-party request. Only explicit active-page Add or Load/Retry grants page-font requests under the policy below. Conversion retains complete cmap and GPOS tables. [§5.3]
- `font` is free text. An unavailable face in the attribute draws in the fallback; an unavailable rich-text font tag is shown literally, as in game. The preview explains each case separately, including pending/settled page delivery; unknown ordinary faces report in game since 0.11.102. Export keeps exact names and remains available. [§5.3]
- Local bytes stay in the browser. The editable name is lookup/display identity; family, slot and italic come from the async metadata reader. A manual-name import whose metadata cannot be read may use slot 0 after browser validation, with a warning. Imports/removals refresh layout and diagnostics. [§5.3]
- Page fonts are direct FONT src="…" defs, not a second project list. Op/key reads are case-insensitive; src is a strict non-empty string, exact duplicates request once in declaration order. Whitespace-only/malformed/blocked links remain source/export data; the preview refuses them without fetching. FONT outside direct DEFS reports unsupported. [§4] [§5.3]
- Add or Load/Retry explicitly requests only the active page, with a Google notice. Open/import/share/example selection, quiet parsing, page switches, undo/redo and export never grant consent; project-provided consent is ignored. Failed/refused links retry only on an explicit action after 60 seconds; no timer fetches. Direct file links remain preview-unsupported and exportable. [§5.3] [§8]
- Fonts mod 0.4.0 is unpublished, with owner-supplied in-game facts: HTTPS only for page URLs and files inside allowed CSS, redirects refused as download-failed; fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com plus their subdomains in game, exact CSS/asset hosts respectively in preview. Direct TTF/OTF load in game; WOFF2 fails there. RequestFontWithReason and the 48 requested-face cap date from 0.3.0; Registrations and refusal before download from 0.4.0. Only 0.2.0 is a confirmed Workshop publish. The deterministic requirements line names each API and its introduction version, with availability depending on the installed build and no publication claim. [§5.3] [§8]
- Face naming uses ID16/17 else ID1/2 per field: Family for empty/Regular style, else Family Style verbatim. Page-variable families without ID16 use ID17, else the instance matching all axis defaults, else ID2 to identify the default style. A non-Regular style or non-400 default wght requires an exact space-plus-non-Regular-style suffix on ID1 and a nonempty family after removal; otherwise refuse naming. Eight more served families (24 native-read files at 400;700, six non-Regular defaults) and the original profiles match static TTF names after this derivation: twelve requested-range comparisons across eleven distinct families, including corrected Noto Sans JP. Static/local names stay unchanged. Metadata fvar instances inside the CSS weight range supply variable aliases; ital/slnt match style and every other unselected axis (including wdth) stays at its metadata default. A single weight selects only that instance. Fixed single-weight descriptors and explicit axes select aliases; isolated browser family names keep CSS lookup under registry precedence. One representative per family/style/weight group, exact-URL metadata caching and lazy Unicode subsets avoid an eager subset burst. Missing/mismatched names fail preview with a cause, readable fallback and unchanged export. [§5.3]
- Precedence is player local files > bundled faces > earlier usable page downloads > fresh requests. Local replacement/removal reveals the next usable face; page-link removal retains session faces. Conflict/subset notices expire with their link generation on cancel/final declaration removal; stored-record notices expire on local removal/replacement. Project replacement clears all font notices and late observers, retaining cached faces. Removed/superseded local imports report their actual cause. Local and page faces use Fonts-mod calibration. Metadata runs in an isolated worker with a 60-second boot allowance, a 5-second read watchdog only after ready and the real 512-MB wasm ceiling. Ready workers are reused; the loader forwards its AbortSignal to cancel departed-link/project reads. Failure cannot reset the chip. [§5.3]
- The panel shows project N distinct links across all pages and per-link K unique delivered names/unknown. Game limits are 48 distinct links per process and 48 requested faces per session, shared across pages/projects/consoles. Cached faces cost nothing, a name clash counts, failed/disabled requests do not. These are informational, never preview/export caps or remaining-session estimates. The page Fonts panel and preview warnings are implemented, including active-page click-to-load in both example modes. Export bytes and warnings depend only on declarations/model names; pending/settled delivery notes are preview-only. [§8]

- Supplied browser checks observed no Google request when opening example 19; Load registered Orbitron/Orbitron Bold from CSS plus one representative in 407 ms, the range six aliases and Roboto nine. Noto Sans JP registered its correct two names from one representative of 124 files in 5.7 s. Page CSP blocked an off-host font URL before a request/network entry. Metadata boot measured 120–211 ms (development), 79–89 ms (production); reads 62.7 ms then 2.6 ms. Registration/transport observations do not prove glyph-axis or geometry fidelity. The 2026-10-11 game run captured current example 18 and loaded example 19 (Orbitron/Orbitron Bold), with clean scenes, and saw Panel wells/Bars. Shadow/hairline comparisons and example 19 fallback remain untested (font already cached). Captures stay outside the repository. [§5.3] [§8]

## Page shapes
- A page shape is a real surface's px and design units: 480 per cell of the console's height (a vertical page's width), the other side keeping the px aspect; a custom size is 480 on its shorter side. A shape of an existing aspect still gets its own entry when its units differ. Shape keys are saved in projects and never change. [§5.4]
- The MoreConsoles shapes (owner, 2026-10-06): 2×2, 3×2 and 3×3, flat and monitor, and each one turned vertical, monitors included (the 2×1 monitor too). Each px is the console's surface size rounded to whole px (owner, 2026-10-06: not `ui:size()`'s fraction), one shape per distinct rounded size (plain and Dual share one), each its own picker entry (owner, 2026-10-06: the real sizes differ); §5.4 marks each as read in game (`ui:size()`) or from the prefab (`ModConsole.ScreenSize`). [§5.4]
- Exports keep one surface per page. In-game rendering and its measurement (FPS, the cost of hidden surfaces) belong to the vector mod, not the builder (owner, 2026-10-11): no single-surface mode, and the builder does not probe rendering cost. [§6] [§10]
- LargeConsoleMod's 2×2 and 3×3 (Steam Workshop; the owner's own) read 460×460 in game: own shapes of 960×960 and 1440×1440 units, no vertical one. [§5.4]
- Measured in game on 2026-10-12: a standard landscape 2×1 console (the same prefab as the others) rotated in the world renders its screen portrait, at 460×1036. The builder's vertical 2×1 shape matches it; that size is the owner's mod's own. [§5.4]

## Widgets (M2b)
- Widgets expand to plain vector nodes, never `SYM`/`USE`. The marker is `# widget <type> <uid> <json params>`. [§2] [§4]
- One state integer per widget: 0 idle, 1 ok, 2 warn, 3 crit. Colours are colour expressions over it, not data colours. Glows use per-state `v=` copies. [§5.1]
- Selection snaps to the widget root (until E1, which lets a person enter a widget to edit its parts); no nested widgets; uids are unique across the project. A pasted or loaded widget whose uid is used already is detached as a copy; one beside its widget gets copies of the `<uid>_` defs it points at (`<uid>copy<k>_`), and a def its page lacks comes from its marker (a `theme_` gradient from the theme). Text its params give but for its colour literals (a palette retuned since, another theme; a text value is compared as written) stays a widget, made again in the theme; "edited by hand" is for real edits only. A load re-associates every page and makes every `theme_` gradient in the theme's colours; a detach pins its samples. [§4] [§5.1]
- No name the mod reads is refused: an expression slot takes `ReadName`'s names, a label-only slot any name its binding reads back (its value a number or a string), a preview value any non-empty name. List items keep their slots by a stable key. [§5.1]
- Any size the mod draws; a height only where a `fit=ellipsis` label needs one line (1.4 × its theme size). A widget that hides everything holds its box with an unpainted `R` (no empty-scene marker). [§5.1]
- Vanilla +: outlines sit inside the box; the hero unit is 64 px. [§5.2]

## Editor
- The catalogue is cross-checked against the mod's `REFERENCE.md`. The tagged REFERENCE correction in 0.11.113 agrees with the code: nofill skips radial and blurred fills as well as labels (since 0.11.105). That disagreement is closed. A code/reference disagreement is reported upstream; a measurement decides which side is wrong. Until then the reference is a provisional tie-break, marked provisional; reference silence is a question, not a defect. Numeric free text (quoted or not) is accepted since 0.11.102; strict strings, colours and enums retain their own readers. A fault the mod reports gets no trap of its own; where the editor already warns of one (an unknown key, a colour, shadow or stop it reports, a clip, symbol, mask or gradient `DEFS` lacks), the warning says the mod reports it. A `mask` without `@` and a clip's further shapes are reported since 0.11.96, a def op in a clip since 0.11.99, and an unreadable numeric format or a letter/%+more format that loses its number under the final 0.11.103/0.11.105 rules; empty formats use the default, so their traps went; a node inside a clip's shape (whose children the mod never parses) stays a trap. [§4]
- The builder is a drawing tool: draw shapes, transform (handles, rotate, nudge), snap (grid, nodes, page; guides; whole units), space (multi-select, align, distribute, exact gap), style (panel, copy/paste, named theme styles), arrange. Milestone M2c, alongside M2b. The owner's final UI design is built (the redesign, 2026-10-12, `docs/ui.md`); a cleanup pass follows once he has worked with it. [§4] [§11]
- Editing tools are open-ended: tools and commands plug into one registry, snapping and layout are pure functions, and a new tool is one file plus its registration. [§4]
- A widget's resize stops at its module's minimum, never crossing over. With Shift, a side handle dragged across the opposite edge stops its axis at the minimum (held where the other reaches its own minimum) and the other axis follows the clamped lead, so the ratio holds. A corner under Shift leads with the axis dragged further for its size, and its following axis never flips, so it never collapses to the minimum. The snap acts where the pointer is, and not at all once the lead has crossed. [§4]
- A named style or a style paste never rewrites a `theme_` gradient def the page has, only adds one it lacks (a hand edit survives them). Only a theme switch refreshes `theme_` defs; loads and widget edits keep their existing behaviour (a load makes every one in the theme's colours; an edit of a widget that paints with one writes it again). [§4]
- Canvas edits never overwrite an expression or $data value: the axis or handle stays and says why; Group (a group of one) is the escape. The exception is a style paste or a named style, which replaces them as the user's explicit act and says how many. Snapped values are whole design units; an object snap within 8 CSS px wins, over wholeness too (an odd width centres on a half unit); Ctrl/Cmd turns snapping off. A Shift resize of a plain node keeps the ratio: on a corner only a line snaps it (the lead lands on it, the follower keeps the ratio) and its sizes are written to 3 decimals, so fractional; on a side handle the dragged side snaps as any edge does and the other side keeps the ratio; a widget's are always whole. The grid step is a whole number of units (0 is off). [§4]
- Boxes are exact geometry: a P's box holds every Bézier and arc extreme, an SP's is its drawn samples, a turned node's the box of its turned geometry; not its stroke, shadow or glow. Handles, outlines, align, distribute, the gap, snapping and the marquee read them; a widget root's box is its params box for all of them. Snap targets are every visible leaf's box and each root block's own box. A group is read at any scale the mod draws; only a singular one is not. [§4]
- Rotation writes G r; L Y SP turn their points; other leaves get a turn wrapper G a r. A group scale writes `s` (6 significant digits, float32) and `t`. Hide is v=0; lock is the comment # lock, which rides with its node; the image placeholder is an R marked # image until M5. Editor settings are per browser. Lock holds the geometric edits; a press sees through a locked node. No id a copy gives holds a native's uid or has a widget's or native's uid as its stem: it becomes `<uid>copy<k>_…` (Q14). [§4]
- Picking follows the game: ztext order, labels paired with their T by geometry, the inside of a shape that draws nothing there picked by its outline; the preview draws a later shape over a label as well (owner, Q6; built as the cover pass). Where label boxes meet, a press takes the label drawn last by its cover, as the preview stacks them; a label whose T is unknown, and every label under ztext=0, is placed by its cover. Missing or non-finite covers leave labels on top; equal covers draw and pick the later frame-list label. [§4]
- One gesture, command or nudge burst is one undo step. Undo brings back the selection from before the edit; redo the set the edited project had when the undo left it. Esc on an open gesture cancels it with no undo step, bringing back the project and the set at its first step (a drawing tool's Esc never leaves the new node's path selected). The status bar (one line) and the Messages drawer keep a fixed height and the selection path is a one-line overlay on the canvas, so the canvas never moves under the pointer. Frames carry the scene they were drawn from; a press reads the node numbering only while that scene has the current shape. [§4]
- The owner answered M2c questions 1-6 on 2026-10-06 and kept the defaults except Q6, plus the whole-unit grid: refuse an expression, never wrap it (Q1); rotation also turns L Y SP and wraps other leaves (Q2); hide as v=0, lock as # lock (Q3); the image placeholder and per-browser settings (Q4); no resize or rotate handles on a multi-selection (Q5; superseded by E1, owner 2026-10-12); the preview covers a label with a shape drawn after it, as the game does (Q6, changed; built). The owner kept questions 8 and 9, from the final review, as built (2026-10-06): on a set, hide flips each member and lock sets all one way (Q8); lock holds only the geometric edits (Q9). [§4] [§11]
- Nothing in the editor's chrome mounts, unmounts or re-wraps when a gesture starts or ends, and every gesture keeps its press's canvas origin and view (2026-10-12; the implementation rule behind "the canvas never moves under the pointer"). An empty shadow list `sh=[]` is no fault: the mod reads it as no shadow, silently (seen in game). [§4]
- Canvas navigation: a plain wheel or a two-finger scroll pans (Shift + wheel sideways); Ctrl/Cmd + wheel and a trackpad pinch zoom at the pointer; Space-drag pans. [§4]
- The fx toggle exists only where an expression is read. Autocomplete is a panel `<datalist>`, no CodeMirror autocomplete. [§4]
- Nothing the mod accepts is refused: a colour it reports (drawn magenta, or a shadow or stop dropped) or reads in silence is a warning, never an error, in the code pane and the panel alike. Traps cover the `SCENE` header too, and a clip, symbol, mask or gradient `DEFS` lacks. A trap's work budget leaves a warning, never a verdict; only the validator's budgets make a scene too large to check. [§4]
- A scene too large to check here lands from the code pane, both panels and the canvas's checked edits (paste, duplicate, group, ungroup, style paste), its reason as information; the preview keeps the last scene it showed. Delete is kept by any button and the bars around the canvas. [§4]
- The `L`/`LS` and open-`SP` fills follow the code, which upstream confirmed at 0.11.94 (the reference is to follow). The mod reports an unknown key on a def since 0.11.94, and on a clip's shape and the header since 0.11.96; the host's keys on the header (`z_index` …) pass unreported and do nothing in src (a not-read trap). `sd` and `sdo` are unknown since 0.11.96. An SC reads its scroll offset from its own map since 0.11.98: a `so` passed down to it, from a G or the SCENE header, is stroke opacity only, so its `sov` needs its own `so`. Nodes inside an `RP` get hit regions (`id:0`, `id:1`, …). Ids and the bindings that are not expressions (`text=$name`, `{$name}`, a paint's `$name[i]`) take any Unicode, supplementary-plane characters included (measured at 0.11.94); a name inside an `=` expression or a `{=…}` placeholder is letters, digits and `_`, and since 0.11.98 any supplementary-plane character too, and the builder reads names so. A `SCENE` `w` or `h` below 1 is a trap: the mod raises it to 1 without a report. So is a point list `p` with an odd number of values (the last is dropped), and an `L`, `Y` or `SP` with too few points for its paints (a stroke takes two, a fill and a shadow three): fewer than two, or all at one place, `p=5` none, draws nothing; with two, a `Y`'s fill and shadow draw nothing, an `L`'s own fill nothing and an `SP`'s own fill at most its feathered edge (a fill passed down to an `L` or `SP` is not counted: provisional, for the owner to rule, since from three points the mod fills it), measured at 0.11.97 to 0.11.101 and seen on a console at 0.11.101 (a two-point `Y`, odd `L`s); so is an entry of a list key that is not a number (read as 0), and a single number on `dash` (a solid stroke). Format reports follow the 0.11.103/0.11.105 rules: empty fmt/placeholder formats use the default; punctuation-only literals are silent, while formats with letters or %+more that lose the number are reported. Float and long routes both get a chance. Final escape scanning and the first colon outside an index apply consistently; %% collapses. Numeric colour expressions, unknown enum words and authored literal-only/opacity/event/radius/unit faults are reported; inherited defaults are not authored keys. A group default passes down only as written, in lower case: `F=`, `SO=` or `S_=` where it is only a default (a `G`, the header, or a node with a block whose op does not read that key) is a trap; the panel reads and writes a default there by its exact key, and src keeps `G F=#0f0 f=#f00` whole. [§4]

## Live data (M3)
- A project's live data: roles, reads, state, click actions, latched rules, bindings ($name → a read's value, state or history; a state variable; an expression; a custom export; a constant; the log ring), custom Lua, simulated devices and settings (budgets, assume beta, day clock, wipe on re-export). Every field has a default; a shared project is sanitized on load. [§4] [§6.1]
- Simulated values live on simulated devices; the preview reads them through `ic.*`, as an export does. [§6.1] [§7]
- Preview source-key changes reload the chip to remove obsolete sources; ordinary source-value changes stay live. A duplicated simulated device gets a new id/ref, an empty label and no pin; added LogicType names must match the catalogue, including its numbered extensions, when it is loaded. Without a catalogue, any non-empty name can be added. [§7]
- Custom Lua keeps its own local scope and is checked for reserved global writes. A page chosen by `U.init` survives boot unless an unresolved required `one` role opens the Devices page first. [§6.2] [§6.4]
- One runtime library (`VB.LIB = 1`) plus generated config; hooks and custom code run unprotected; device calls are pcalled; expressions are Lua 5.2 expressions under a lexical guard. [§6.4]
- Latched rules write on a read-back difference; a write-only type writes on its value's edge; nil holds. Buttons set state, rules drive devices. [§6.2]
- An action may be scoped to a page (raw ids are unique per scene only); a pulse rule may be chosen on any type that must be re-sent on a command. [§6.2]
- Pages build lazily (the start page at load); each has a snapped and an eased data element; a nil sends its kind's default, an empty table is never sent. Histories, counters, text and other nonmoving values snap; only moving geometry eases, including arrays of Bar levels. Known normalized geometry suppresses movement below half a surface pixel, compared with the last sent value so drift accumulates. Automatic colour thresholds and arbitrary raw expressions only discard equal float32 inputs. A shared label snaps; an extra raw reader or widget-root attributes remove the normalized travel bound. Every changed payload rebuilds once; snap prevents glide rebuilds, not that initial rebuild. [§6.3]
- The Devices page is generated from widget modules. Its appearance is the console author's concern; the builder does not force layouts on it. Fixtures and examples are examples for a larger console, and an example's appearance on a console is never a defect (owner, 2026-10-11). A pick is saved by a press, to `ic.persist` and a chip-memory mirror, and boot never writes either. Rows cycle through auto; SAVE of explicit auto clears the saved choice and resets the transient pick. SAVE keeps a role's saved pick while the role has no device on the network; boot opens Devices first whenever a required `one` role is unresolved, whatever else was saved. [§6.1] [§6.3]
- A `one` role's saved pick is matched by ref, then by a label or name hash only when exactly one candidate has it; an empty label or a zero hash is never stored. A store unreadable at boot turns its writes off for the session. [§6.1]
- A role may be marked `distinct: true` (2026-10-12; it acts on `one` roles): it never takes a device another distinct role holds by its pick, saved choice, label, pin or ref; a device two distinct roles reach the same way goes to neither; the Devices page's tap-to-pick skips a device another distinct role holds. Roles without it are unchanged. The Airlock starter's two door roles use it, so one airlock on the network never binds both. [§6.1]
- Reference ids are never visible to the player (owner, 2026-10-12: "those are never in any way visible to the player"): no player-visible text shows a chip, device or network reference id. The generated Devices page rows show names only, e.g. "Gas Sensor" or "Tank (2 share this label)"; ids stay internal. In the editor (owner's choice for the UI redesign, 2026-10-12), the role's ref field moves behind a collapsed "Advanced" disclosure labelled "Pin to one specific device", the only place the id shows, and saved projects keep their value; the simulated-devices panel's "ref N" and the write log's "ref N:" become device names (Editor UI). [§1] [§6.1]
- `ic10-devices.md` cross-checks the catalogue's export: an md device, hash, pair, access or slot type the export does not hold alike stops the build; it also names the vanilla devices. An optional in-game enum probe cross-checks `ic.enums` and numbers a mod's LogicTypes. [§3] [§10]

Empty batches give Average NaN, Sum 0, Minimum 0, Maximum -Infinity, Count 0 (P3); enum numbers match the catalogue (P7). [§7]

Preview wipe-on-re-export defaults off; save/reload timing remains untested (P5b). [§6.1] [§7]

- Embedding defaults on, with a browser preference and explicit off choice. The whole project, custom Lua, simulated setup and font names/URLs travel; chip stores, font bytes, consent and delivery state do not. [§8] [§10]
- One versioned transfer codec: `1.<base64url(gzip(UTF-8 project JSON))>`, no padding. JSON and builder blocks restore through the existing sanitizer without evaluating surrounding Lua. A fake marker inside a string does not qualify; the last genuine top-level block wins. Foreign-script adopt/its offer remain M5, with retry and JSON rescue in M4. Files read as Blobs in the transfer worker, never as a copied textarea; Stop terminates a pending read and retains the original file. [§3] [§6.5] [§8]
- Share URLs use only `#p=` and are offered only at **8,000 characters for the entire absolute URL**. Overlong links fall back to JSON, never truncation. A token over 7,997 characters cannot come from this producer and is rejected before decoding. Gzip ISIZE must fit three times measured engine string limit L; output exceeding its claim is corrupt and decoded text exceeding L is a platform limit. These are real producer/format/platform boundaries, not an arbitrary builder project/depth cap. [§8] [§10]
- Remove p before decoding to prevent a reload loop. A successful read restores the token until that same replacement generation is durably saved, including later edits; consume only its still-current fragment after commit. Content rejection leaves p removed and offers explicit choices; transient failure restores it with a reload warning. Rejected hashchange input never pauses current-project autosave. Keep the original link/file outside the tab for rescue. [§8]
- IndexedDB keeps one current serialized project plus one displaced-current recovery record. Startup reads before preview/save and prefers an unconsumed valid fragment, then current, then Blank, with empty restore history and a complete runtime reset. Save committed identity after 500 ms quiet, FIFO/coalescing with transaction completion and optimistic revisions. First successful save of each explicit replacement rotates once; edits/undo/redo keep recovery. Conflict/unavailable storage visibly pause with JSON rescue; no revision stealing or silent overwrite. Pagehide is best effort. [§8]
- Isolated preflight compiles/loads once, parses every exact manifest source and visits/renders all generated pages, Devices included, for two dt=0.5 ticks each and at least 20 ticks total. Cold stores and simulated inputs; no clicks, real physics, TMP shaping or Fonts delivery guarantee. At most 16 distinct Scene.Problems per scene, with missing/starved/dropped separately disclosed. Runtime text has no guessed node/key; only real structured locations link. [§8]
- Known Lua/scene/platform errors and provable conditional generated persistence loss block; accepted mod faults, unknown fonts/gradients, missing names, small text, starvation/drops and observed persist refusal warn. Oversized init, uncertain future writes and speculative totals warn, not inevitable loss. Nothing the mod accepts is refused. Watchdog/cancel/crash means incomplete; only explicit labelled Export unchecked with zero known errors can acknowledge unfinished checks, and Retry/JSON remain available. [§8]
- Ten starters follow both provided themes: seven square-console ones (Overview, Atmos/tanks, Power, Airlock, Solar, Blank and the plain-shape Needle gauge) and three of the owner's own consoles rebuilt from the builder's widgets at their consoles' shapes, never resized: Power wall (3×2) and Power wall (2×3), the two layouts of his power console with the same logic, and Hydro farm (3×2) (owner, 2026-10-13: he chose "Rebuild each from builder widgets", "do not resize them", and both Power layouts as two templates; the mapping and what could not be built are in docs/templates-power-farm.md). Airlock (owner, 2026-10-12: "yes it should kinda match that", matching his own working airlock console script) opens one door at a time, only once the other reads shut and settled (Open 0, Idle not 0, 1.5 s after its close command); an unbound door counts as shut.
